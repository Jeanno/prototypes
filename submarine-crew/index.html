Tap a station below to take that seat. Everyone on this code shares one boat. Captain, Comms, Helm and Navigator are live; the rest are still mockups.

Notes on the crew split

  1. Deck gun alone idles. Most play is submerged. Weapons 2 runs the torpedo room (reloads, flooding, muzzle doors) and mans the gun only on the surface.
  2. Fire control builds the shot. Weapons 1 turns Sonar's track into a firing solution, then fires and wire-guides. Firing waits on the Captain's permission and the torpedo room.
  3. Helm owns depth too. Steering only would be thin next to the Navigator. Helm runs course, speed, planes, trim and ballast.
  4. Comms is tied to depth. No reception below 60 ft. Every broadcast or contact report pulls the boat shallow, toward danger. That's the Captain's dilemma.
  5. The Captain gets hands. A request queue to rule on, the periscope, standing orders. Otherwise they only talk.
  6. Engineering shares one budget. Power splits the battery, Life support and Damage control both spend it, and every pump or fan adds noise to Sonar's counter-detection.
